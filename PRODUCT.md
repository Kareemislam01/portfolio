## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Kareem for software engineering / AI roles, plus a broader professional audience: peers, collaborators, and contacts arriving via LinkedIn or network referral who want a fuller picture of his background than a resume gives.

## Product Purpose

Kareem Mohamed's personal portfolio. It presents his real experience, education, and skills so a visitor — recruiter or otherwise — comes away with an accurate, memorable understanding of his background and can reach out easily.

## Positioning

Demonstrated breadth, not a single narrow lane: data analysis (Excel, SQL, VBA), front-end work (HTML/CSS/JS UI updates), and core software/AI engineering (Siemens Industrial Copilot), on top of an avionics-systems integration background (Bombardier) and a Computer Engineering degree. The range across data, front-end, and software is the differentiator versus a candidate who has only ever done one of these.

## Operating Context

A static personal site. Visited by recruiters/hiring managers during sourcing or screening, and by broader professional contacts researching Kareem before or during a hiring conversation or after a LinkedIn connection.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No backend, no auth, no data collection. Contact is a `mailto:` link and a LinkedIn link — no forms.
- Content is sourced from Kareem's LinkedIn export (`kareem.islam2001@gmail.com`'s profile PDF). All experience, dates, and bullets must trace back to that source or a later confirmation from Kareem — never invented.
- Projects section is a known open gap: three cards (a COEN 320 real-time-systems course project, a conversational chatbot, and his Computer Engineering capstone) are placeholders marked "Details pending" in `src/data.ts`. Real descriptions, tech stacks, and links are still needed before this section is complete.
- `profile.github` in `src/data.ts` is intentionally empty pending Kareem's GitHub URL.
- Phone number is deliberately omitted from the public Contact section for privacy; email + LinkedIn only.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Kareem Mohamed. Voice: plainspoken, understated, a little dry humor (per his own LinkedIn summary), professional throughout — never oversold or corporate-sounding.

## Evidence on Hand

- LinkedIn profile export (`Kareem Mohamed`, Concordia University B.Eng Computer Engineering 2019–2025) — source of truth for all experience and education content currently on the site.
- No testimonials, case studies, press, or third-party proof on hand. Do not fabricate any.
- Real project artifacts exist locally (a COEN 320 project, a chatbot project, a capstone project) but are undocumented; do not invent scope, stack, or outcomes for them.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim on the site traces to verifiable fact (LinkedIn, resume, or a direct confirmation from Kareem) — never fabricate achievements, metrics, employers, or projects.
2. Show range: data, front-end, and core software/AI work all deserve visibility, not just the most recent role.
3. Contact stays frictionless — one click to email or LinkedIn, no gatekeeping.
4. Personal contact details stay minimal and private by default (no public phone number); only what's needed for someone to reach out.
